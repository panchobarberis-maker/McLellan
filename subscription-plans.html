Ongoing Legal Support

At McLellan Law Group, LLP, we understand that legal issues don't just show up when it's convenient, or inexpensive. That's why we offer flexible monthly legal support plans designed specifically for California entrepreneurs, employers, and real estate investors who want to stay proactive, protected, and legally prepared.

Our subscription tiers provide ongoing access to legal guidance you can trust.

Whether you're building, hiring, expanding, or resolving challenges behind the scenes, we're here to help you navigate it all, with clarity, consistency, and the legal foresight that protects what you've built.

Please note: Litigation services (including representation in court or arbitration) are not included in any monthly plan and require a separate written fee agreement.
