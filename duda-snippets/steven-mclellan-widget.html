About

"Steven McLellan brings tenacity and strategic clarity to every case, from trial courts to appellate courts across California."

He has the knowledge and experience to help clients get a favorable result whether filing a lawsuit or defending against one. His practice focuses on business litigation, real estate disputes, financial elder abuse, and landlord-tenant matters.

During law school, Steven externed with the Honorable Patricia Lucas and the Honorable Peter Kirwan at the Superior Court of California, Santa Clara County, and later worked at an international law firm handling securities and intellectual property litigation.
