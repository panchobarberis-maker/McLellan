What is a Guardian Ad Litem?

A guardian ad litem (GAL) is appointed by the court to represent the best interests of a child or adult who lacks capacity in a lawsuit. The GAL investigates, advocates, and makes recommendations to the court to protect the person's well-being and legal rights. Our role is to be a compassionate advocate while providing the court with an informed perspective.

Who Needs a Guardian Ad Litem?

Guardians ad litem are often appointed for children in cases involving family disputes, personal injury, or abuse. We also serve adults who are incapacitated, elderly, or otherwise unable to make informed legal decisions regarding a lawsuit. Our firm ensures that their interests are safeguarded at every step of the legal process.
