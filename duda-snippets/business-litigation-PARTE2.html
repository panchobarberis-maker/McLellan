Partners, corporate officers and directors, and certain LLC members and managers can owe each other duties of loyalty and care, though whether a particular person owes them depends on the entity, the governing documents, and that person's actual role in the business. Where the duty exists, a breach occurs when someone in a position of trust puts their own interests ahead of the business, through self-dealing, diverting opportunities, misusing company funds, or making unauthorized decisions. Whether a duty was owed in your situation is usually the first question we answer, because it determines everything that follows.

Type of claimDeadlineWhen the clock generally starts

These are general periods. Which one applies to a particular claim depends on how the claim is pleaded and on facts specific to your situation, and more than one period can apply to the same set of events. If you are close to a deadline, that is a reason to call now rather than a reason not to.
